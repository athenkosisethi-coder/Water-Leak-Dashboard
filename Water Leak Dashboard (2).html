Leak records

Map coordinates: 22 records had errors in the source register, such as a longitude of 18.24 for Hamba Street (Khayelitsha High Level) or a latitude of -34.22 for Micha Frankhamed (Khayelitsha Low Level). Swapped values were swapped back, single-digit typos were corrected where the fix lands inside the zone, and otherwise the bad value was replaced with the zone's central value. Corrected leaks have a dashed outline on the map; hover to see what changed. Estimated loss is the source register's L/h figure. Water loss implication is the register's own calculated field and has not been validated as verified savings. Repair status is derived from the register's notification and verification fields. Category spellings were standardised (for example Firehydrant and Fire Hydrant).
